IMAGE SUPPORT
The quiz already supports an imageName field in QuizQuestion.
Example:
QuizQuestion("Look at the diagram...", QuizQuestion.Type.MCQ,
    listOf("10","20","30","40"), "20", "diagram01")

Put the image in app/src/main/res/drawable/ as diagram01.png.
To actually display it, add an Image(painterResource(...)) block in QuizScreen.
The current starter version keeps the question data ready for images.
